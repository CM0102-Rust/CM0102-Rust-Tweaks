# CM0102 Rust Tweaks: the phrases of the youth intake message (intake_news = 1).
# Keep this file next to cm0102.exe. Edit any phrase, or translate a language: the message is written when it is
# shown, in the language the game is set to (Game Options > Language), so changes apply to messages already in the
# inbox too. Without this file, or without a phrase, the DLL uses its own English, Portuguese and Spanish ones.
#
# One section per game language, [CODE], with the game's own codes:
#   ENG_UK, ENG_US, FRA, DEU, ITA, ESP, DAN, NED, NOR, POR, SVE, JAP, KOR, POL, RUS, CHI_SIM, CHI_TRA, POR_BRA, ESP_MEX
# Lines are "key = phrase". The words in braces are filled in by the DLL:
#   {n}          how many youngsters arrived (title, short)
#   {name}       the youngster's name (player)
#   {age}        his age (player)
#   {position}   goalkeeper, defender, midfielder or forward, from the phrases below (player)
#   {now}        the coach's view of his current level, now_0 to now_3 (player)
#   {potential}  the coach's view of his potential, potential_0 to potential_4 (player)
#   {ca} {pa}    his current ability and potential, only with intake_news_numbers = 1 (numbers)
# title: the headline. intro: the first line of the body. player: one paragraph per youngster. numbers: added to the end
# of the paragraph with intake_news_numbers = 1. short: the line stored inside the message, shown if the save is opened
# without the DLL (up to 67 characters; it's stored in the language of the intake day).
# now_0: up to 5 points below the average current ability (CA) of the club's best 11; now_1: 6 to 20 below;
# now_2: 21 to 40 below; now_3: more than 40 below.
# potential_0: PA 170 or more; potential_1: 150 to 169; potential_2: 130 to 149; potential_3: 100 to 129;
# potential_4: below 100.
# The game shows Western European letters (Latin-1); a letter outside it shows as "?".
#
# Este arquivo traz as frases da mensagem da fornada. Edite ou traduza à vontade; a seção usada é a da língua do jogo.

[ENG_UK]

player = {name} ({age}, {position}): {now} and {potential}.
numbers = (CA {ca}, PA {pa})
short = Youth intake: {n} youngsters joined from the youth team.
goalkeeper = goalkeeper
defender = defender
midfielder = midfielder
forward = forward
now_0 = is already ready for the first team
now_1 = could get chances in the first team soon
now_2 = needs a year or two in the youth team
now_3 = is still very raw and will take time
potential_0 = could become one of the best in the world
potential_1 = has the potential to play for his country
potential_2 = could be a regular at a big club
potential_3 = should make a career in the top flight
potential_4 = is unlikely to go beyond the lower divisions
